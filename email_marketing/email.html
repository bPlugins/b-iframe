New free plugin

Embeds that stop breaking on phones

Hi there,

You are getting this because you use one of our plugins, and we just released something we think you will actually use: iFrame, a free plugin for putting videos, maps, playlists, design files and whole websites inside your pages, in iframes that behave.

Paste a link, get a working embed

No more hunting for "embed URLs". Paste a normal YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, Spotify, Loom or Figma page link and iFrame rewrites it to the form that actually plays. YouTube even switches to the privacy friendly no-cookie player.

It fits every screen

Pick an aspect ratio (16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, 21:9 or your own) and the embed scales with the page instead of overflowing on mobile. Your desktop layout and your phone layout finally agree.

Build once, embed everywhere

The ShortCode Generator gives every iframe its own shortcode, like [b-iframe id="123"]. Paste it into posts, widgets, Elementor or your theme. Edit in one place, it updates everywhere.

Fast and search friendly

Embeds are rendered server side, so search engines see them, nothing jumps while the page loads, and the script we ship to your visitors is a few KB of plain JavaScript. The editor even warns you when a site refuses to be embedded, before your visitors meet an empty box.
